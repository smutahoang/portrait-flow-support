Images show the production app screens and artwork, rendered in Android layouts. Full guided access is enabled for demonstration. Billing, ads and storage are isolated during capture. Captions frame complete app images. No prices or third-party ads are staged.

Artwork previews and free drawing are free. Selected beginner guides may be unlocked with an optional rewarded ad. Premium unlocks full guidance and removes ads while active. See the listing copy and published Baby Can Draw support page for access and privacy details.

Store entry, declarations, subscription configuration, a signed release build and Google review are still required before publication.
