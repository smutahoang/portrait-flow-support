Small lines. Big smiles.

Discover the joy of drawing with Baby Can Draw. Pick a minimalist artwork, watch each stroke take shape, then try it yourself on screen. A calm place for beginners, children and the parents, older siblings and grown-ups who draw alongside them.

FROM “WHERE DO I START?” TO YOUR FIRST DRAWING
• Follow animated, stroke-by-stroke lessons.
• Pause, replay and move between strokes at your own pace.
• Trace the guide or practise with hints and adjustable guide visibility.
• Explore free drawing when you want to make something your own.

A LITTLE COLLECTION TO EXPLORE
Start with a simple heart, a happy sun or a sleepy cat. Discover 26 built-in drawings across animals, nature, everyday objects and more. Preview every artwork and see its difficulty and number of strokes before you begin.

TOOLS THAT KEEP DRAWING SIMPLE
Use your finger or stylus. Choose a brush size, undo a wobbly line, redo a change and zoom in when you need a closer look. Left-handed controls and reduced-motion settings help you find a comfortable way to practise.

KEEP YOUR HAPPY LITTLE DRAWINGS
Your work saves to a gallery on your device. Come back to unfinished practice, revisit what you have made and export drawings as PNG or SVG when you are ready to share.

DRAW WHEREVER YOU ARE
The artwork collection is included in the app, so browsing, previews and basic drawing work offline. Ads, purchases, restoration and subscription verification need an internet connection.

START FREE, WITH OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Browse all artwork previews and enjoy free drawing. Selected beginner guides can be unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded ad. Premium subscriptions unlock the full guided collection and remove ads while active. Monthly and annual plans are available; Google Play shows the price and billing period before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage or cancel them in Google Play.

MADE FOR DRAWING TOGETHER
No app account is required. Drawings stay on your device unless you choose to export or share. Purchase actions have a grown-up gate, and ads use child-directed treatment for all ages. Children should use the app with guidance from a parent or trusted grown-up.

Currently available in English.
