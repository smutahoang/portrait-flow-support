Support

Drawing and saved work

Your drawings, custom guides, lesson progress and preferences are stored on your device. Keep exported copies of drawings you want to preserve. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; exports saved elsewhere remain under your control. Operating-system backups may also contain app data.

Premium purchases

Use Restore purchases on the Premium screen with the store account that made the purchase. An internet connection is needed for purchases, restoration and subscription verification. You can manage an iOS subscription in Apple subscription settings or an Android subscription in Google Play subscription settings. The store where you purchased handles payments and refund requests.

When reporting a problem

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version and a description of what happened. Share a screenshot only if useful, after removing personal information. Do not send passwords, full payment details or identifying information about a child.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Applies to Baby Can Draw

Baby Can Draw is published by Phapi Group. This policy explains the app's local storage and its use of Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat and Google AdMob. Contact anha2k47@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Drawing stays on your device

No app account is required. The app does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, email address, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access. On Android, a neutral first-use screen asks for an age range, including a 'Prefer not to say' option. This choice is kept only in local settings and is not sent to us, the advertising SDK or the purchase provider. The same child-directed advertising and grown-up purchase protections apply to every choice. Drawings, imported or custom guides, lesson progress, settings and access records are stored locally. The app does not upload your drawings to our servers. When you choose export or share, the file is passed to the destination you select using the device's share controls.

Subscriptions and purchase validation

Apple processes iOS payments and Google Play processes Android payments. We do not receive your card details. RevenueCat verifies purchase receipts and subscription status so the app can unlock Premium and restore purchases. It processes purchase and transaction information, product identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, currency, locale and an app user identifier. Regular use starts with an automatically generated anonymous identifier. We do not supply your name or email address to RevenueCat. For store review on Android, a reviewer can optionally enter an access code that identifies a dedicated review profile in RevenueCat. This links that installation to the review profile so its granted Premium access can be verified. Entering a review code does not start a subscription, charge a payment or create an automatically renewing trial. RevenueCat also provides subscription reporting and analytics. This purchase processing is separate from optional drawing-activity analytics, which are disabled for this family audience.

Optional rewarded advertising

Free users may choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock an eligible guide. Premium members do not receive ads. The app uses Google AdMob and applies child-directed ad treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G to every user. An Android age-range choice stays on the device and does not change those protections. Personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled through that treatment.

To deliver ads, measure their operation and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and operating-system information, app or developer-bounded identifiers, ad views and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. Child treatment disables transmission of advertising identifiers such as IDFA and Android advertising ID. We do not request precise location permission or use an advertising tracking permission prompt. Child treatment limits advertising data use; it does not mean that ads involve no data processing.

Google's consent service checks which privacy choices are required before the app requests ads. Where a privacy-options entry is required, you can reopen it in Settings. If ads are unavailable, no purchase is made automatically.

Children and families

The app supports children with guidance from family members. A grown-up gate appears before an in-app purchase, and the store requires its own purchase authorization. We do not provide public profiles, chat or a public gallery. Parents can supervise sharing and contact us about data associated with their child's use.

Support, retention and choices

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send, and use them to respond. Do not include sensitive information about children. You can delete saved drawings in the app and remove other local app data by uninstalling. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately. Providers may retain purchase, security and advertising records under their own policies and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact us for help with access or deletion requests; purchase records may need to be retained for legal, payment or fraud-prevention reasons.

We do not sell your drawings or use them to train AI models. No custom advertising audiences or cross-app advertising integrations are configured in the app. Service providers may process information in other countries. For their practices, see RevenueCat's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

This support website

This page contains no analytics scripts or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process technical request information; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Subscriptions and terms

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Baby Can Draw provides on-screen drawing practice. A grown-up should supervise purchases, external links and sharing when a child uses the app.

Free access and the introductory period

Artwork previews and free drawing are free. During the first 72 hours after first successful launch, one optional rewarded ad unlocks an eligible free guide for the rest of that introductory period. After it ends, a rewarded ad can unlock an eligible free guide for each new lesson session. Premium-only guides require active Premium access throughout. The introduction is not an automatically renewing subscription trial: it does not enroll you in a subscription or charge you.

Premium

Premium unlocks the guided collection and removes ads while the subscription is active. The U.S. base prices are USD 3.99 per month or USD 24.99 per year. Local prices, taxes and available offers may differ. The store's purchase screen shows the price, period and any applicable offer before you authorize payment.

App Store subscriptions (iOS)

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation, and renewal is charged within 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal; access normally continues to the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles refund requests under its policies.

Store purchases and restoration need an internet connection. Offline Premium access relies on a recently verified status and may require reconnection. Use Restore purchases if an existing purchase is not recognized.

Artwork and your creations

You retain your rights in drawings and other content you create or import. Import only content you own or have permission to use. The bundled artwork is provided for practice within the app; it is not an assignment of ownership or a license to redistribute the source collection.

Google Play subscriptions (Android)

Android subscriptions are charged to your Google Play account on confirmation. Each plan is billed for its full stated period and renews automatically unless canceled before renewal. Manage or cancel in Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal; access normally continues until the end of the paid period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Google Play handles refund requests under its policies. The store purchase screen shows the current local price, billing period and any offer before purchase.

iOS license

Use of the iOS app is subject to Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. This page describes the app's access and subscription features and does not replace that agreement or your statutory consumer rights.

Report an inappropriate ad

A grown-up can email anha2k47@gmail.com with the approximate time, country, device type and advertiser or ad description. Include a screenshot only if safe, with personal information removed. Please do not ask a child to click an ad to investigate it.

If the ad has an information or reporting control, a grown-up can also use that control to report it to Google.
